OEM procedure audit
SRC 466TA / 466NA

2026-10-24 · Deliverable A · Companion workflow revision 3

Audit outcome: NOT RELEASED for unrestricted shop use. Use SRC for documented specifications/instructions and the original IH manual wherever SRC is silent. Actual NG component differences and data missing from both sources remain subject to verification. Ten verification subjects remain open; V02 is resolved by the source hierarchy. SRC specifications take precedence over IH wherever the two differ; where SRC is silent, use the original IH manual for compatible mechanical instructions, as directed by the project owner; superseded IH values are comparison evidence only. The delivered workflow is operational software with explicit blocked procedures; it is not evidence that the unresolved engineering work is complete.

Evidence and scope

Four linked documents were read through the authenticated Drive connector. IH (481 PDF pages) and SRC (43 PDF pages) were also downloaded for page-index and figure checks. Printed IH 1-81 / PDF 106 and SRC p17 were visually inspected for head sequences and valve groups. The live measurement HTML was retrieved and its source inspected on 2026-10-24. Source hashes below identify downloaded copies. The distributed checklist copy now includes only section/return navigation additions; the live repository file is not changed. Existing checklist contents, calculations and save functions are retained.

This is an applicability/procedure audit for the companion workflow, not a claim to have validated every pre-existing measurement formula or numerical limit. The IH compilation covers 300 and 400 series, numerous diesel variations and later bulletins; matching the family name does not prove every component matches the SRC build.

The workflow order is a TSI integration of the cited OEM component operations: evidence before disturbance; head/valvetrain access before tappets/cam; pan and cover before gear measurements; pistons before sleeves/crank; clean/machine/inspect before fitting; matched bearings and measured thrust before final closure; timing/backlash before front cover; sleeve/ring/rod acceptance before head; lash/rotation/prelube before first start. OEM component removal/install prerequisites are retained. Independent components can have multiple physically valid orders; the manuals do not provide an SRC-specific, bare-block whole-engine sequence. No invented sequence is described as verbatim OEM.

Verification register — Deliverable C

Ten items below are OPEN at delivery; V02 is resolved by the project source hierarchy. Required closure evidence must identify the installed build and supply the resolved applicable instruction; closing a software checkbox does not confer manufacturer approval.

V01 · Engine and mechanical revision identification

Source: IH 1-34, 1-57–59, 1-84–90; SRC 18–19

IH small-journal crank dimensions differ from later DT466E sheet. SRC lists narrow bearings and an NG head/piston; a measured journal alone does not establish all specification compatibility.

Required: Record serial, block/head/crank/cam casting and part numbers, journal diameter AND width, bearing marks, rod/bolt types; obtain applicable OEM build/parts and service specification confirmation.

V02 · Head procedure source hierarchy — resolved

Source: IH 1-59, 1-81–82; SRC 33

Resolved by project instruction: use SRC final head torque 170 ft-lb; use original IH procedure for details absent from SRC. Use IH intermediate stages 110 and 155 ft-lb with sequence A, then SRC 170 ft-lb with IH sequence B. Bolt lubrication, washer identification, .005-in rocker bracket gauge, first-stage rocker freedom check and first-hour retorque/lash follow-up use IH 1-59,1-81–82. This is a traceable combination of sources under the project rule; no single manual prints the combined 110/155/170 schedule.

Required: No additional SRC document is required solely because SRC omits the procedure. Confirm fitted head/bolt/gasket identity under V01/V03; retain actual torque records. The unchanged measurement sec-6 still has 165 ft-lb, so cross-reference the actual 170 record rather than falsely attesting its old checkbox.

V03 · Natural gas piston, rings, pin and head limits

Source: SRC 18–19; IH 1-60–62, 1-125–130, 1-139–145, 1-152–153

SRC N104108 is an NG 8.5:1 piston; N104011 is an NG head. Diesel ring profiles, gaps, groove limits, piston protrusion, pin fit and valve machining values are not automatically approved for these parts.

Required: SRC or component manufacturer drawing/service sheet for fitted piston/ring/liner/head/valves, including gaps, fits, recession, head thickness/resurfacing allowance and ring orientation.

V04 · Valve lash temperature and cover torque

Source: SRC 16–17, 31–33; IH 1-82

SRC states .025 in but does not explicitly state hot/cold in the cited adjustment procedure. Existing checklist labels it warm. SRC p17 says 36 in-lb; p33 says sheet metal 26 in-lb, aluminum 40 ±10 in-lb.

Required: Confirm lash temperature condition and installed cover/grommet tightening specification. Do not silently assume warm or select one cover value.

V05 · TA ignition timing and controls commissioning

Source: SRC 30–32

TA town gas is 27° BTDC; pipeline, wellhead and landfill entries are TBD. NA values are separate and are not transferable. Diesel injection timing does not set spark timing.

Required: Applicable SRC ignition system installation/indexing, cylinder/lead routing, timing curve and setting method for serial, fuel analysis and controller; shutdown/overspeed verification procedure.

V06 · Fuel specification and pressure disagreement

Source: SRC 7; generator data sheet fuel table

SRC says at least 70% methane or 950–1100 BTU/ft³; data sheet says methane 90% minimum and lower heating value 890 BTU/ft³ minimum. Data sheet mixer pressure 3.5–5.5 in H2O is application specific.

Required: Confirm fuel composition, pressure measurement point, loaded/unloaded limits, regulator/lockoff/mixer setup and approved gas leak test with SRC for this build.

V07 · Run-in, cam bedding and load rating

Source: SRC 8–11; IH 1-100, 1-154–155; generator data sheet

SRC first 100 h: half to three-quarter load, no maximum load >5 min; refers to 100 kW. Data sheet has 125 kW prime. IH cam recommendation 1000 rpm for 3–5 min may conflict with constant-speed genset controls. Existing load-bank schedule is not an OEM-approved substitute.

Required: Written rebuilt-engine/cam run-in plan, applicable electrical and engine rating, load steps/durations, temperature/oil pressure limits and head retorque integration; approve test equipment and follow-up log.

V08 · Pressure tests: source unit discrepancies

Source: IH 1-195; SRC 15–16

IH oil cooler text pairs 935 kPa with 150 psi; these are not equivalent. Do not choose either silently. System pressure and induction test limits depend on installed assembly.

Required: Confirm test pressure, medium, temperature, restraint, duration and acceptance criteria for cooler, head, cooling and intake assemblies; calibrated protected test rig.

V09 · Damper and seal assembly variants

Source: IH 1-106–109, 1-182–183; SRC 18, 33

SRC pulley value 105 ft-lb agrees with IH rubber damper; IH viscous hub is 125 and damper screws 35 ft-lb. Seal kits may have different lubrication/installation instructions from the old IH lip seal. Historical asbestos glove instruction must not be followed. SRC 105 ft-lb governs over the original checklist sec-6 pulley 125 entry; verify hardware/method applicability, not a different default torque.

Required: Identify damper and seal kit; obtain installed-part instructions, seal depth/tool/adhesive/lubrication and torque applicability. Use modern rated heat PPE.

V10 · Accessory hardware and missing procedures

Source: SRC 18–30, 33; IH 1-53, 1-185–202

SRC turbo mounting unit is printed 35 LB/LBS; OPR wording is resolved by IH 1-198 identifying the threaded regulator. Fan, flywheel coupling, starter, alternator, ignition and fuel hardware lack complete applicable instructions here. SRC oil-pan 20 ft-lb takes precedence over IH 15 ft-lb; that numerical difference is resolved. The original checklist sec-6 lists drain plug 33 ft-lb; SRC p33 27 ft-lb governs instead.

Required: Applicable accessory/coupling manuals and fastener identification; clarify turbo torque unit; obtain remaining fastener, sealant, pre-lube pressure and electrical/normal oil-pressure test specifications. Oil-pan final torque is SRC 20 ft-lb.

V11 · Block, liner and bearing family release

Source: IH 1-135, 1-138–139, 1-146–151, 1-172–180; SRC 18,33

IH prohibits block deck resurfacing (1-172) and sleeve honing (1-146). Main housing bore limits and liner seal arrangement must match installed block. OEM general fit procedures do not authorize arbitrary machining.

Required: Applicable block/liner/bearing dimensions and seals, supported cap/line-bore repair plan if needed, machine-shop measurements and final disposition. Document main/rod clearances for actual crank family.

Review and release limitations

The downloadable file is self-contained and has no third-party scripts or API keys. Source/checklist links need internet and any Drive permissions.
